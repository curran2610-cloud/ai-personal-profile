# PPT Motion Design

## Style Prompt

Create a restrained internal-training motion deck for a Chinese short-video operations team. Keep the source PPT layout as the visual ground truth, and add only purposeful motion: calm entrances, soft page-to-page wipes, subtle focus accents, and steady pacing that helps employees follow the logic. The result should feel like a polished course video, not a flashy promo.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0f1115`
- Paper: `#f7f3ec`
- Ink: `#151515`
- Accent red: `#d94a38`
- Warm gold: `#c4934b`

## Typography

- Chinese/system fallback: `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, sans-serif
- Latin/support: `Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif

## Motion Rules

- Use slide screenshots as the layout authority.
- Every slide enters with image opacity, slight scale, and accent motion.
- Use soft wipe transitions between slides.
- Keep overlays thin and non-destructive; never cover dense table content for more than a brief transition.
- Avoid decorative particle effects, spinning objects, aggressive zooms, and default blue/purple gradients.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not rewrite the PPT copy inside the video layer.
- Do not animate every table row individually in the first pass.
- Do not introduce unrelated stock imagery.
- Do not use jump cuts between slides.
- Do not let text overlays compete with the source slide text.
